Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the `memepump` server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. Every tool is read-only.

1. `Is this Solana token a rug? Mint: EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v`
2. `Does EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v still have an active mint authority or freeze authority?`
3. `Who holds most of EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v, and do any wallets look linked?`
4. `Check these three mints and tell me which has a honeypot signal: <mint1> <mint2> <mint3>`
5. `Explain what a freeze authority means in a token safety report and what the report cannot show.`
